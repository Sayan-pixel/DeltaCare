## 1. User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Framework

User Acceptance Testing evaluates DeltaCare from the perspective of the end consumer and insurance broker, ensuring that the software fulfills the business objectives defined in [`docs/PROJECT_CHARTER.md`](../PROJECT_CHARTER.md).

UAT scenarios are expressed in executable Gherkin syntax matching [`docs/ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md`](../ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md).

---

## 2. Formal UAT Scenarios

### UAT-01: Anonymous Consumer Quoting Experience
```gherkin
Feature: Anonymous Price Discovery
  As a health-conscious consumer shopping for health insurance
  I want an instant price estimate without surrendering my phone number or email
  So that I can evaluate plan costs without being harassed by sales agents

  Scenario: Consumer receives accurate instant estimate with monthly priority
    Given the consumer navigates to the public DeltaCare web portal
    When the consumer enters their age as 38
    And selects biological sex as "female"
    And adjusts their BMI to 24.8
    And specifies 1 dependent child
    And sets tobacco smoker status to "no"
    And selects their geographic region as "northeast"
    And clicks "Get Instant Quote"
    Then the system must display an estimated monthly premium within 1.5 seconds
    And the monthly price must be visually prioritized over the annual total
    And the system must display a confidence interval range (e.g., "$310 – $370 / mo")
    And zero personal contact details (name, email, phone) were requested
```

### UAT-02: Lifestyle Modification "What-If" Exploration
```gherkin
Feature: Lifestyle Financial Incentives
  As an individual who currently smokes
  I want to see exactly how much quitting smoking would reduce my insurance costs
  So that I have a clear financial incentive to improve my health

  Scenario: Smoker observes dynamic annual savings delta callout
    Given an anonymous consumer has entered an actuarial profile with smoker set to "yes"
    When the quote calculation resolves on the screen
    Then an animated savings callout badge must appear
    And the badge must read: "Quitting smoking could save you ~$X,XXX / year"
    When the user changes the smoker toggle back to "no"
    Then the savings badge must smoothly animate out of view
```

### UAT-03: Legal Transparency & Non-Binder Protection
```gherkin
Feature: Statutory Underwriting Disclaimer
  As a prospective policyholder
  I must be clearly informed that this tool provides statistical estimates rather than official contracts
  So that I do not mistake an informational estimate for a binding underwriting binder

  Scenario: Disclaimer visibility
    Given any generated quote result view
    Then the official underwriting disclaimer must be visible directly beneath the price card
    And the copy must match: "Estimates are generated by a machine learning model for informational purposes only. This does not constitute an official quote, offer, or insurance binder. Actual premiums are determined by underwriting."
```
